BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY
Policy reference HR-320
1. Scope. This policy applies to all salaried employees.
2. Approval. Requests under this policy must be approved by the operations supervisor before taking
effect, and no more than 3 time(s) per week without escalation.
3. Notice. Requests must be submitted at least 14 business days in advance through the HR portal.
4. Review. This policy is reviewed annually by Human Resources.
