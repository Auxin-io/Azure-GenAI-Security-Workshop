DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Policy reference HR-422
1. Scope. This policy applies to all salaried employees.
2. Approval. Requests under this policy must be approved by the department manager before taking
effect, and no more than 1 time(s) per week without escalation.
3. Notice. Requests must be submitted at least 4 business days in advance through the HR portal.
4. Review. This policy is reviewed annually by Human Resources.
